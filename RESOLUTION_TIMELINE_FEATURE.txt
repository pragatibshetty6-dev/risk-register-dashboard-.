RESOLUTION TIMELINE FEATURE

Each risk should record:
- Created At: when the risk was registered
- Last Updated: when the record was last changed
- Status: Open / In Progress / Resolved
- Resolved At: exact date and time when status became Resolved
- Resolved By: person/team that resolved it
- Resolution Method: how it was resolved
- Resolution Duration: time from Created At to Resolved At

Example:
Created At: 19-Aug-2026 10:30
Last Updated: 20-Aug-2026 14:10
Resolved At: 20-Aug-2026 14:10
Resolved By: Cybersecurity Team
Resolution Method: Access controls were strengthened and the security assessment was completed.
Resolution Duration: 1 day 3 hours 40 minutes

This creates a clear audit trail and lets the dashboard show when and how every risk was closed.
